I run a dedicated Snowflake proxy to help people in censored countries reach the open internet. This badge
does the same thing right here in your browser tab. While this page is open, it volunteers a small amount of
your bandwidth to that same network. Closing the tab stops it; nothing persists, nothing is installed.
