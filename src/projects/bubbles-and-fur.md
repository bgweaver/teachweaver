A site for a home cleaning business, built once and then rebuilt a few months later when I knew more. It has
an interactive service-area map, a contact form that pre-fills based on which service you clicked, and PayPal
integration for booking. The redesign is the version that's live now. Comparing the two is a decent record of
what a year of practice does.
