I lost my wedding ring, so I made a new one out of a quarter minted the year we got married. Punch the center,
heat it, and hammer it down around a mandrel until the face of the coin folds into the outside of the band.
The first one came out rough but wearable. I've got four more quarters from the same year, on the theory that
I will absolutely lose this one too.
