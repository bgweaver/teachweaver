A Proxmox host and a storage tower, both built from secondhand and e-waste hardware, running the services I'd
otherwise rent from someone else. Segmented VLANs, my own DNS, my own backups. It is never finished and that
is entirely the point. Most of what I've learned about networking and Linux came from breaking this and then
having to fix it before anyone noticed.
