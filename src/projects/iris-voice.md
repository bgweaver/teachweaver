A voice assistant for the house that answers to "Hey Iris" and never talks to Amazon or Google. Speech
recognition and synthesis both run on my own server, so a command spoken in the kitchen stays in the
building. Satellites are a mix of bought hardware, ESP32 boards I flashed, and one repurposed Echo Show. The
wake word was the hard part: off-the-shelf models fired at the TV constantly, so I trained a custom one.
