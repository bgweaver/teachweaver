A Discord bot that collects alerts from everything running in my homelab into one place. Before it existed,
notices about updates, backups, and failed services arrived by email, text, and three separate apps, which
is a good way to notice none of them. Net-chan has an anime-server-assistant personality and a bad attitude
about being asked to do things, which makes routine maintenance notifications marginally more entertaining
than they deserve to be.
