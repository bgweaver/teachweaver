A daily Indianapolis sports digest for people who don't follow sports but have to talk to people who do.
Each item pairs a real result with a question you could actually ask someone. Data comes from ESPN's free
endpoints; the LLM handles phrasing only and never touches a fact, because a confidently wrong score is
worse than saying nothing.
